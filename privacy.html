Privacy Policy — Keep the Light

Effective date: November 3, 2026

Keep the Light ("the Game") is published by AugustInnovations LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the Game handles.

Information we collect

The Game collects no information and shares no information. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs and no third-party tracking, and it does not sell anything about you to anyone.

Data stored on your device

The Game saves your progress (the current century, headlands completed and your settings) in your device's local browser storage so you can pick up where you left off. This data never leaves your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it.

Purchases

The Game has no in-app purchases. Everything in it is included in the app you download.

Network access

The Game makes no network requests. All of its files are included in the app, and it plays entirely offline. Updates to the app come through Google Play like any other app.

Children

The Game does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
